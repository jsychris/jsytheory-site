JSY Theory

Support

JSY Theory is a study app for Jersey's driving theory test — car and motorcycle, in English and Portuguese.

Common questions

  • How do I switch language or study mode? Profile tab → Language (English/Português) and Study mode (Car/Motorcycle).
  • How do I reset my progress? Profile tab → Reset all progress. This only affects your device — the app stores nothing anywhere else.
  • Does the app cover the hazard perception part of the test? No — it covers the 50 multiple-choice questions. Practise the video clips with the official materials.
  • Is this an official DVS app? No. It is an independent study aid based on the Jersey Highway Code and the UK Highway Code. Rules change — always check gov.je before your test.

Report a problem or a content error

Spotted a wrong answer, a typo, or a bug? Please open an issue here with the question text or a screenshot — content corrections are treated as the highest priority and ship in the next update.
